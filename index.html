Who We Are

The GENA Initiative advances global public health through the development of open-source computational tools and scientific platforms that democratize access to genomic and epidemiological intelligence. We also undertake high-impact open scientific research, including structural modeling of viral proteins to support vaccine effectiveness studies, investigation of pathogen evolution, pandemic preparedness, and biosecurity using publicly available datasets from GISAID, NCBI, and other global repositories.

Vision

To create a world where AI and computational tools drive rapid, data-informed public health decisions, enabling resilient and responsive health systems worldwide.

Mission

To design, implement, and deploy open-source pipelines and AI-driven public health software, making advanced analytics, pathogen genomics, and predictive modeling accessible and actionable for real-world health challenges.

Our Strategic Pillars

Open-Source Innovation

Developing open-source computational tools, AI solutions, and scientific platforms that democratize access to genomic, epidemiological, and bioinformatics intelligence, particularly for researchers and public health institutions in low- and middle-income countries.

Open Science & Discovery

Conducting high-impact, reproducible scientific research using publicly available datasets (e.g., GISAID and NCBI) to investigate pathogen evolution, viral genomics, structural biology, vaccine effectiveness, and emerging infectious diseases.

Global Health Security & Preparedness

Translating genomic and epidemiological insights into actionable evidence that strengthens pandemic preparedness, biosecurity, outbreak response, and public health decision-making through collaborative, data-driven approaches.

Training & Capacity Building

Strengthen global scientific capacity by providing accessible training, mentorship, and educational programs in genomics, bioinformatics, computational biology, AI, and data science. Foster a skilled workforce through workshops, fellowships, open educational resources, and collaborative learning networks that empower researchers and public health professionals, particularly in low- and middle-income countries.
