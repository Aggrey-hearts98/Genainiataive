Biography

Shahid Rowles-Khalid is a British Research Scientist specialising in Virology, Genomics, Artificial Intelligence, and Global Public Health.

His interdisciplinary profile combines computational virology and genomic science with neural machine learning and generative artificial intelligence. This combination supports the exploration of computational approaches to biological and public-health challenges.

Shahid's work reflects the growing convergence between biological sciences and artificial intelligence, with an interest in using advanced computational methods to generate scientific insights and support global health research.

"Bridging computational science, genomics, artificial intelligence, and global public health to advance scientific discovery."

His scientific interests also extend to the intersection of global public health and international health initiatives, including work connected to the broader global health ecosystem and the United Nations.
